- **Equal allocation never reaches the 0.08 target** (best LCB 0.0786 at
  48 scenes) and *declines* beyond 48 — the equal-allocation bias
  diagnosed in E8/E10, now reproduced on an independent dataset whose
  largest regime holds 42% of scenes.
- **Proportional allocation reaches 0.08 at 32 scenes and 0.20 at 40
  scenes**, dominating uniform at every size (e.g. 48 scenes: LCB 0.260
  vs 0.189).
