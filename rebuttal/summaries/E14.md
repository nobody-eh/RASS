## Honest reading

- The certified minimum budget at p_min = 0.08 does not move (36 both ways).
- At exactly 48 scenes the two allocations are statistically comparable
  (82 vs 88 passes; LCB 0.168 vs 0.182) — on this mildly skewed population
  equal allocation is not badly biased, unlike the heavily skewed
  cross-method (E10) and DL3DV (E13) populations.
- From 60 scenes upward proportional dominates decisively (96sc: LCB 0.400
  vs 0.241), pulling the stricter 0.20 operating point from 96 to 60 scenes.
- Nothing undermines RASS-48/96: the released artifacts are export-audited
  scene lists, allocation-independent by construction; equal allocation is
  the documented generator of record.

## c. Hold-ready reply (345 chars)

> No. RASS-48/96 are export-audited scene lists, allocation-independent;
> equal allocation is the documented generator of record. Proportional
> keeps the 0.08 budget at 36 scenes, is comparable at 48 (LCB .168 vs
> .182), and dominates beyond (0.20 target: 60 vs 96 scenes). The revision
> reports both allocations and adopts proportional going forward.
