# E10: E1 rerun with proportional allocation (Task P10)

Symmetric allocation reporting for the three-method joint event. Identical
to E1 in every respect (3,473-scene intersection, same references, 16
constraints, paper tolerances, KS ≤ 0.14, INGP per-frame-mean PNSR, M = 400,
rng = default_rng(seed + b)) except the per-regime allocation: proportional
to regime size with largest-remainder rounding (allocations recorded per
budget in the JSON). The equal-allocation evaluator was validated to
reproduce E1's frontier exactly ([1, 5, 6, 14, 20, 20, 30, 27]) before the
proportional run.

## Verdict sentence (119 chars, rebuttal-ready)

> Proportional allocation certifies at 72 scenes where equal allocation
> does not - reported for symmetry, not preference.

## a. Frontiers side by side (n_pass/400, Wilson LCB)

| Scenes | 24 | 36 | 48 | 60 | 72 | 84 | 96 | 120 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proportional | 0 | 8 | 17 | 36 | **49 (.094)** | 67 (.134) | 85 (.175) | 122 (.262) |
| Equal (E1) | 1 | 5 | 6 | 14 | 20 (.033) | 20 (.033) | 30 (.053) | 27 (.045) |

## b. Targets

- Proportional: LCB ≥ 0.08 at **72 scenes**; LCB ≥ 0.20 at **120 scenes**.
- Equal (E1): neither target reached up to 120 scenes.

The honesty note in the task anticipated similar behavior on this
"near-balanced" intersection; the data says otherwise, plainly: the
intersection's regime sizes ({724, 417, 457, 715, 528, 632}) are unequal
enough that equal allocation carries a systematic bias against the
population references, and removing that bias (proportional) roughly
quadruples pass counts at large budgets. This strengthens the E9 narrative:
the allocation effect is real on every population, not an artifact of the
skewed-coverage intersections.

## c. Binding constraints at 48 scenes (383 failing candidates, proportional)

Same ordering as E1's equal-allocation histogram — mean constraints
dominate, led by LPIPS: zipnerf.lpips.mean 268, zipnerf.ssim.mean 216,
feature_splatting.lpips.mean 203, feature_splatting.ssim.mean 176,
instant_ngp_fi.ssim.mean 163, zipnerf.psnr.mean 162; KS constraints all
lower. LPIPS-tolerance tightness (see E11) is allocation-independent.
