# E3: sensitivity and calibration (Task P3)

Serves rTZt W3, 6aTr Q2, p5AG W4, AC. Zip-NeRF event on the full audit
population (3,521 effective scenes), balanced generator, M = 400, seed
protocol rng = default_rng(audit_seed + b). Fast KS evaluator validated
(reproduces 88/400 at b=8, 113/400 at b=16). Clustering replication
validated (shipped k=6 seed-0 labels reproduced exactly, ARI = 1.0, using
the 57 descriptors minus the 3 budget proxies).

## One-sentence stable conclusion (drafted from what the numbers show)

> The audit's calibration is internally consistent (every tolerance sits at
> 4.5–8% of the corresponding per-scene std and below every cross-method
> gap, and the KS guardrail 0.14 sits below the alpha=0.05 critical distance
> 0.197 at n=48), and the recommended budget is stable at 36–48 scenes in a
> neighborhood of the default tolerances and across audit seeds at the
> published clustering (36 in 5/5 audit seeds at K-Means seed 0); however,
> the certified budget is sensitive to the regime definition itself — under
> re-clustering with other K-Means seeds the recommendation spreads over
> 36–120 scenes (median 48), at k in {4,5,8,10} the 0.08 target is not
> reached within 120 scenes, and the exported 48-scene subsets are far from
> unique across audit seeds (mean pairwise Jaccard 0.005) — so the honest
> claim is stability of the budget *scale* under the published protocol, not
> uniqueness of the subset or invariance to regime redefinition.

## c. Calibration numbers

- KS critical distances (alpha = 0.05, N = 3,522): n=36 → **0.2275**,
  n=48 → **0.1973**, n=96 → **0.1405**, n=120 → **0.1261**. (Sanity values
  ~0.197 at 48 and ~0.140 at 96 confirmed.) The paper's KS ≤ 0.14 guardrail
  is *stricter* than the 0.05 critical distance at 48 and ~equal at 96.
- Full-set per-scene stds: PSNR 6.2681 dB, SSIM 0.1684, LPIPS 0.2244.
  Tolerances as fractions of std: PSNR 0.0798, SSIM 0.0594, LPIPS 0.0446.
- Smallest cross-method full-population mean gap on the 3,473 intersection:
  PSNR 0.7469 dB (FS vs INGP), SSIM 0.0199 (Zip vs FS), LPIPS 0.0952
  (Zip vs FS, the only LPIPS pair). **Every tolerance is below every method
  gap** (0.5 < 0.7469, 0.01 < 0.0199, 0.01 < 0.0952) — the paper sentence is
  supported with margin.

## a. 4×4 tolerance sweep — recommended budget at target 0.08

Rows tau_PSNR (with tau_SSIM = tau_LPIPS = 0.02·tau_PSNR), columns tau_KS.
Identical candidate draws across all 16 cells (reused stored trial stats).

| tau_PSNR \ tau_KS | 0.10 | 0.12 | 0.14 | 0.18 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | not reached | not reached | not reached | not reached |
| 0.375 | 72 | 48 | 48 | 48 |
| 0.50 | 60 | 48 | **36** | 24 |
| 0.75 | 48 | 36 | 24 | 24 |

Monotone in both axes; the default cell (0.5, 0.14) → 36 validates against
the full sweep. Within the neighborhood {0.375–0.75} × {0.12–0.18} the
recommendation stays in 24–48 scenes; halving all tolerances (0.25 row)
makes the target unreachable below 120.

## b. Regime count and seeds

Recommended budget at 0.08 per k (budgets = multiples of k nearest the
standard grid; shipped mappings for k ∈ {4,6,8,10}, all verified ARI = 1.0
against the replication; k=5 newly clustered, seed 0):

| k | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recommended | not reached ≤120 | not reached ≤120 | **36** | not reached ≤120 | not reached ≤120 |

**k = 6 is not incidental**: with the same audit, other regime counts do not
reach the 0.08 LCB target within 120 scenes. This supports the paper's k
choice but should be stated as such (the pass event is calibrated to the
k=6 stratification).

5×5 seed grid at k=6 (K-Means seed × audit seed), distribution of
recommended budgets over 25 cells: 36 → 7, 48 → 8, 60 → 4, 72 → 2, 84 → 1,
96 → 1, 120 → 1, not reached → 1. At the published K-Means seed 0 the
recommendation is **36 for all five audit seeds**; re-clustering with other
seeds shifts it upward (median over the 25 cells: 48).

Mean pairwise Jaccard of the best 48-scene subsets across audit seeds 0–4
(K-Means seed 0): **0.0053** — the exported subsets are essentially
disjoint across audit seeds. The certified object is the budget and the
audit procedure, not a unique subset; many near-equivalent 48-scene subsets
exist (consistent with ~22% of all balanced candidates passing).

## Caveats

- Stds computed on the effective 3,521-scene population (documented
  off-by-one vs the stated 3,522; the KS critical formula uses N = 3,522 as
  instructed — the difference is < 1e-5).
- k=5 has no shipped mapping; it was clustered with the validated
  replication recipe. All other k use the repo's shipped mappings.
- Seed-grid cells other than K-Means seed 0 re-cluster the population, so
  regime populations and hence the event itself change; the spread mixes
  clustering variability with audit sampling variability by design.
- Instant-NGP enters part c only (via cross-method gaps, PNSR convention).
