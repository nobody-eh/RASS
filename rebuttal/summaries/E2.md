# E2: ranking preservation added to the formal joint event (Task P2)

Serves reviewer 6aTr Q3. Same population (3,473-scene intersection), same
references, same generator, and **identical paired candidate draws** as E1
(rng = default_rng(seed + b), seed 0, M = 400; pairing verified by exact
match of joint pass counts at every budget). Ordering constraints: for the 3
method pairs among {Zip-NeRF, Feature-Splatting, Instant-NGP full image} and
each of {PSNR, SSIM}, the subset must reproduce the sign of the
full-intersection mean gap (variant A), and additionally match its magnitude
within 0.5 dB / 0.01 (variant B).

## Quotable headline

> Adding the six cross-method ordering constraints to the joint fidelity
> event is free: every candidate that passes the 16-constraint E_joint also
> preserves all pairwise method orderings at every budget (conditional
> ordering preservation 100% at 48 scenes; variant A is identical to E_joint
> across the sweep), and 82.5% of *all* balanced 48-scene candidates
> preserve the full ordering even without any fidelity screening. Requiring
> gap-magnitude preservation on top (within 0.5 dB / 0.01) roughly halves
> the pass count (48 scenes: 3 vs 6 of 400; 96 scenes: 21 vs 30), and no
> budget up to 120 scenes reaches the 0.08 LCB target for either variant —
> as for E_joint itself. In short, distributional fidelity is the binding
> requirement; once it holds, cross-method ranking preservation follows for
> free.

- Budget targets: LCB ≥ 0.08 and ≥ 0.20 not reached by either variant up to
  120 scenes (variant A best LCB 0.0530 at 96; variant B 0.0346 at 96).
- Conditional ordering preservation at 48 scenes: P(all 6 orderings |
  E_joint) = **6/6 = 100%**. Unconditional: 330/400 = **82.5%**.

## Reference gaps being preserved (full intersection, A − B)

Zip-NeRF − Feature-Splatting: +1.5601 dB PSNR, +0.0199 SSIM.
Zip-NeRF − Instant-NGP: +2.3070 dB PSNR, −0.0631 SSIM.
Feature-Splatting − Instant-NGP: +0.7469 dB PSNR, −0.0830 SSIM.
(Instant-NGP SSIM exceeds both others on this population, and its PSNR is
lowest — signs are non-trivial, so preservation is not vacuous.)

## Caveats

- The unconditional 82.5% here is the probability that a random balanced
  48-scene subset preserves **all six** orderings jointly — a stricter event
  than the paper's 91.0% per-comparison diagnostic; the two numbers are
  consistent but not the same quantity and should not be equated in the
  rebuttal text.
- Conditioning is on only 6 E_joint-passing candidates at 48 scenes (30 at
  96, also 100% order-preserving), so the "100%" is exact for these draws
  but based on small counts; the variant-A = E1 identity across all 3,200
  trials is the stronger statement.
- Instant-NGP PSNR uses the per-frame-mean `PNSR` column, as in E1 and the
  paper's cross-method table.
- Seeds: global 0; per-budget rng = default_rng(0 + b), identical to E1
  (pairing asserted programmatically).
