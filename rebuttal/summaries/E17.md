# E17: DL3DV second method — splatfacto/3DGS (Task P17)

**Status: RUNNING, 4/140 scenes at last check, zero failures.**
**CAMERA-READY MATERIAL ONLY — nothing in the posted rebuttal may cite this,

## Setup

The E13 streamed design with nerfacto swapped for splatfacto: `ns-train
splatfacto` defaults, `--machine.seed 0`, downscale 4 (960P), COLMAP-seeded
Gaussian initialization, per-scene PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS in the E13 log schema
with a provenance field marking it camera-ready. Resumable; scenes are
claimed atomically on the shared filesystem so heterogeneous GPUs
self-balance from one queue, and a failed scene releases its claim.

Running on a 2-GPU node (H100 NVL 94 GB + H200 NVL 140 GB), 6 workers.

## Measured performance

17.9–18.0 min/scene/worker **including** ns-eval; ~3 min/scene effective
with 6 workers, so ~7 h for 140 scenes. The bottleneck is the scheduler's
16-CPU-per-node cap on the GPU partition (splatfacto undistorts ~381 images
per scene on CPU), **not** the GPUs: 7 GB of 94/140 GB used, and adding a
third worker per GPU left aggregate throughput flat (~15k steps/min either
way). The partition with 112 CPUs permits zero GPUs, so no configuration on
that cluster lifts the cap.

## Honest expectation for the two-method audit

The between-method gaps (~4–5 dB PSNR, 0.16–0.22 SSIM) are far larger than
the DL3DV tolerances (tau_PSNR = 0.2014 dB under the E3c rule), so the
two-method joint event is a genuinely demanding test: a subset must track
two very differently-behaved distributions at once. Expect a certified
budget **at or above** nerfacto's 32 scenes. To be reported either way.
