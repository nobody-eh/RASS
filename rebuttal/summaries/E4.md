# E4: regime-level fidelity budget (Task P4)

Serves reviewer p5AG W3. Event: Zip-NeRF global event (paper tolerances,
global per-metric KS ≤ 0.14) AND per-regime mean constraints
|mean_m(S∩r) − mean_m(full∩r)| ≤ c·tau_m over the six k=6 regimes (18 mean
constraints), c = 1 and c = 2 as separate events. Balanced sampler on the
full audit population (3,521 effective scenes), budgets 48–360, **M = 400 at
every budget** (fast evaluator; no reduction was needed), seed 0,
rng = default_rng(seed + b). Validation: the global event reproduces 88/400
at 48 scenes before the regime constraints are added.

## Headline (honest)

> Regime-level mean fidelity is not achievable by balanced sampling at any
> audited budget: with per-regime tolerances equal to the global ones
> (c = 1), 0 of 400 candidates pass at every budget from 48 to 360 scenes,
> and even with doubled tolerances (c = 2) at most 4 of 400 pass at 360
> scenes — the 0.08 LCB target is not reached below 360 scenes for either
> event, so the paper's guarantees should continue to be stated at the
> global level, with per-regime reporting kept diagnostic.

## Frontier

| Scenes | 48 | 72 | 96 | 120 | 180 | 240 | 360 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c=1 pass | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| c=1 LCB | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| c=2 pass | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| c=2 LCB | 0 | .0004 | 0 | 0 | .0004 | .0014 | .0039 |

Smallest budget with LCB ≥ 0.08: **not reached up to 360** for both c = 1
and c = 2. The c=2 trajectory is monotone-ish but tiny (0.0039 at 360).

## Why (useful for the rebuttal text)

This is a statistical inevitability, not a defect of the generator: the
per-regime PSNR std is of order the global std (~6.3 dB), so a regime
sub-sample of b scenes has mean standard error ≈ 6.3/√b dB — at b = 60
(360 total) that is ≈ 0.8 dB, still above the 0.5 dB tolerance, and all 18
constraints must hold simultaneously. Certifying per-regime means at the
global tolerances would require on the order of several hundred scenes *per
regime*. "Not reached below 360 scenes" is therefore an honest prescription:
regime-level certification is a different, much more expensive guarantee
than global certification.

## Caveats

- KS constraints stay global only, per the task spec; the binding failures
  are the per-regime mean constraints.
- M = 400 was used at all budgets including above 240 (recorded in
  `m_used_per_budget`); runtime was not a constraint.
- Population is the effective 3,521-scene audit set (documented off-by-one).
