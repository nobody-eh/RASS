# E8: audit extended with nerfacto and bionerf (recovered cluster logs)

New per-scene logs recovered from the student's cluster scratch (2026-07-24):
**nerfacto 2,762 scenes** (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS), **bionerf 2,620 scenes deduped**
across three run dirs (fine-network metrics; priority
output_json_bionerf > output_json5 > output_json6). Intersections:
I4 = E1 ∩ nerfacto = **2,728**; I5 = I4 ∩ bionerf = **2,092**. Same
tolerances, balanced k=6 generator, M = 400, seed 0 as E1.

## Headline (honest, with diagnosis)

> Extending the joint event to nerfacto (and bionerf) currently yields
> 0/400 passes at every budget — but this is a coverage artifact, not an
> audit failure: the recovered logs cover only 78% (59%) of the audit set
> with missingness concentrated in specific regimes (63–71% retained in
> regimes 0/1/2/4 vs 96–97% in 3/5), which shifts the balanced generator's
> target mean 1.29 dB PSNR / 0.030 SSIM / 0.042 LPIPS away from the
> intersection references — several times each tolerance. The audit is
> behaving correctly by refusing to certify under population shift; a fair
> 4/5-method extension needs the remaining ~760 nerfacto (~1,430 bionerf)
> scenes to finish training, after which E8 reruns unchanged.

## Results as run

- **E_joint4** (Zip-NeRF, Feature-Splatting, Instant-NGP, nerfacto; 22
  constraints, population 2,728): joint 0/400 at every budget 24–120.
  Single-method rates *decline* with budget (e.g. Zip-NeRF 14/400 at 48 →
  1/400 at 120) — the population-shift signature (variance shrinks around a
  biased mean).
- **E_joint5** (+ bionerf; 28 constraints, population 2,092): joint 0/400
  everywhere; only bionerf's own event is insensitive (62/400 at 120)
  because its bias happens to be small for its own metrics.
- RASS-48 on I4: only 37/48 scenes lie in I4; FAIL (feature_splatting
  psnr+lpips, nerfacto psnr+ssim). RASS-96 on I4: 73/96 scenes, **PASS all
  22 constraints** (selected subsets are more robust to the shift than
  random candidates). On I5 both fail.

## What this is good for in the rebuttal

- Evidence the audit detects non-random missing coverage rather than
  blessing it — a robustness talking point, if used carefully.
- Do NOT present E8 pass rates as the multi-method result; E1 (96–99%
  per-regime retention) remains the formal event.

## Caveats

- nerfacto/bionerf logs are from in-progress training campaigns; the
  missingness is plausibly correlated with scene properties (regimes), as
  the retention table shows.
- bionerf metrics use the fine network (fine_psnr/ssim/lpips); dedup
  priority recorded in the JSON inventory.
- All seeds and the generator protocol identical to E1
  (rng = default_rng(0 + b), M = 400).
