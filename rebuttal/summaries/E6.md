# E6: descriptor-group ablation + DINOv2 variant (Task P6)

Serves p5AG W4/Q2, 6aTr Q2. Zip-NeRF event, paper tolerances, balanced
generator, M = 400, audit seed 0 throughout.

## One-sentence overall finding

> The certified budget is robust to the descriptor representation — an
> entirely different, self-supervised embedding (DINOv2 ViT-S/14) yields
> the same recommended budget of 36 scenes and a similar frontier
> (pass-rate 0.20 at 48 scenes vs 0.22 for the 57-D descriptors) — while
> the group ablation shows no single descriptor group suffices and the full
> 57-D set outperforms every alone/leave-one-out variant, so the hand-
> crafted descriptors are jointly useful but not privileged: the audit
> certifies budgets, not a particular feature engineering.

## a. Group ablation (k = 6 recluster per configuration, coarse grid 48–120)

Mapping (57 dims, grounded in `src/feature_extractor.py`): capture_pose 21,
image_stats 4, texture 2, mask_geometry 8, sparse_geometry 22. Budget
proxies always excluded from clustering (validated protocol). Column
subsets of `feats_normalized.csv` are exactly the same-pipeline
renormalization (per-feature-independent transforms).

| Configuration | dims | rate@48 | LCB@48 | rec. budget (coarse) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **full_57d (reference)** | 54 | **0.2200** | **0.1822** | 48 |
| minus_capture_pose | 36 | 0.1675 | 0.1341 | 48 |
| texture_alone | 2 | 0.1500 | 0.1183 | 48 |
| mask_geometry_alone | 8 | 0.1475 | 0.1161 | 48 |
| minus_sparse_geometry | 32 | 0.1400 | 0.1094 | 48 |
| image_stats_alone | 4 | 0.1175 | 0.0895 | 48 |
| sparse_geometry_alone | 22 | 0.1050 | 0.0786 | not reached ≤120 |
| minus_mask_geometry | 46 | 0.0975 | 0.0721 | not reached ≤120 |
| minus_image_stats | 50 | 0.0900 | 0.0657 | 120 |
| capture_pose_alone | 18 | 0.0025 | 0.0004 | not reached ≤120 |
| minus_texture | 52 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | not reached ≤120 |

Full set is best; every ablation degrades. Two instructive quirks, both
re-clustering effects (consistent with E3's seed sensitivity): capture-pose
alone produces near-useless regimes, and *removing* the 2 texture dims
collapses the event entirely (the 52-D clustering shifts to a partition
whose equal-allocation mean misses the references). The coarse grid starts
at 48, so "rec. 48" cells may actually admit 36 (untested here).

## b. DINOv2 variant (completed, not deferred)

DINOv2 ViT-S/14 (torch.hub), 8 uniformly-sampled frames per scene,
224×224, CLS mean-pooled, L2-normalized → 384-D per scene; all 3,522
scenes embedded (0 skipped), KMeans k=6 seed 0 on the embeddings, audit
population 3,521, regime sizes {766, 454, 339, 568, 387, 1007}.

| Scenes | 24 | 36 | 48 | 60 | 72 | 84 | 96 | 120 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DINOv2 pass | 17 | 52 | 80 | 91 | 94 | 114 | 133 | 149 |
| DINOv2 LCB | .027 | **.100** | .164 | .189 | .196 | .243 | .288 | .327 |
| 57-D pass (ref) | 26 | 58 | 88 | 92 | 95 | 101 | 113 | 120 |
| 57-D LCB (ref) | .045 | .114 | .182 | .191 | .198 | .212 | .241 | .257 |

**Recommended budget at p_min = 0.08: 36 scenes — identical to the 57-D
descriptors.** DINOv2 regimes are slightly weaker at small budgets and
*stronger* at large ones (133 vs 113 at 96; 149 vs 120 at 120).

## Caveats

- Group mapping judgment calls (documented): aabb_scale → capture_pose;
  sharpness/entropy → image_stats rather than texture.
- DINOv2 clustering is KMeans directly on L2-normalized embeddings
  (declared choice; no re-standardization).
- Embedding protocol is deterministic (linspace frame indices, no random
  augmentation); audit seeds identical to the main sweep
  (rng = default_rng(0 + b)).
- E6.a used a coarse budget grid {48, 72, 96, 120}; E6.b used the standard
  grid {24, ..., 120}.

`rebuttal/e6b_dinov2_embeddings.npz` (3,522 × 384), scripts
`scripts/rebuttal_e6a_group_ablation.py`, `scripts/rebuttal_e6b_dinov2.py`.
