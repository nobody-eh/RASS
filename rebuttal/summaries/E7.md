## Gate verdict

Per-scene PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS for the DL3DV-140 benchmark could not be verified
as publicly available for any method, let alone two:

- **Official GitHub** (DL3DV-10K/Dataset): benchmark results exist only as
  figure images (`imgs/nvs-benchmark-*.jpg`); the only CSV is a
  scene-validity list. No per-scene metric files anywhere in the tree or
  issues.
- **Paper + CVPR 2024 supplement**: Table 3 is explicitly averages over all
  140 scenes; the supplement has per-category bar charts and qualitative
  figures only.
- **Hugging Face `DL3DV/DL3DV-Benchmark`** (gated, auto-grant): full tree
  enumerated via API — per-scene folders hold only training inputs
  (gaussian_splat/, nerfstudio/). The single candidate file,
  `benchmark-meta.csv`, is advertised as containing "the performances of
  methods reported in the paper" (5 methods) but returns 401 without a
  logged-in HF account, and every public consumer of it reads only its
  `hash` column — so whether it is per-scene or aggregate is unverifiable
  from here.
- **Leaderboards/third parties**: no DL3DV-140 leaderboard; third-party
  per-scene releases cover ≤6 scenes or one method (3DGS checkpoints).

**Escape hatch (2 minutes with your HF login):** open
`https://huggingface.co/datasets/DL3DV/DL3DV-Benchmark/resolve/main/benchmark-meta.csv`
after accepting the license. If it holds per-scene metrics for ≥2 methods,
the verdict flips to PASS and the whole study becomes log-based (descriptor
extraction + audit machinery are ready).
