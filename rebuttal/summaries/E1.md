# E1: formal joint multi-method fidelity event (Task P1)

Serves reviewers p5AG (W1, Q1), rTZt (W2), MU2f. Population: the 3,473-scene
common intersection; every reference mean and empirical CDF recomputed per
method on this intersection. E_joint = 8 mean + 8 KS constraints (Zip-NeRF
PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS, Feature-Splatting PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS, Instant-NGP full-image
PSNR/SSIM; object-centric excluded). Tolerances 0.5 dB / 0.01 / 0.01,
KS ≤ 0.14. Balanced generator on k=6 regimes restricted to the intersection,
M = 400, seed 0, rng = default_rng(seed + b). Instant-NGP PSNR uses the
per-frame-mean `PNSR` column (the paper-table convention, per P0).

## Quotable headline

> On the 3,473-scene cross-method intersection, the exported RASS-96 subset
> passes all 16 constraints of the formal joint fidelity event (mean and KS
> tolerances for every available method-metric pair), and RASS-48 passes 14
> of 16, failing only Feature-Splatting LPIPS (mean gap 0.021 vs tolerance
> 0.01; KS 0.165 vs 0.14) — the same gap already reported transparently in
> the paper's cross-method table. Randomly drawn balanced candidates rarely
> satisfy the joint event (6/400 at 48 scenes, 30/400 at 96 scenes), and the
> joint Wilson LCB does not reach the 0.08 reliability target at any budget
> up to 120 scenes, quantifying that jointly matching three methods'
> distributions is substantially harder than matching any single method
> (single-method LCBs at 96 scenes: Zip-NeRF 0.25, Feature-Splatting 0.21,
> Instant-NGP 0.57). The binding-constraint analysis shows the mean
> tolerances — led by LPIPS (Zip-NeRF 281 and Feature-Splatting 269 of 394
> failing candidates at 48 scenes) — are the dominant bottleneck, not the KS
> guardrails.

## b. Reliability targets

- LCB ≥ 0.08: **not reached at any budget up to 120 scenes** (best 0.0530 at 96).
- LCB ≥ 0.20: not reached.

## c. Exported subsets vs E_joint

- **RASS-48** (48/48 scenes in the intersection): **FAIL — 14/16 constraints
  pass**; only Feature-Splatting LPIPS violates (mean 0.0211 > 0.01,
  KS 0.1651 > 0.14). Full 16-row table in `rebuttal_results.json["E1"]`.
- **RASS-96** (94/96 scenes in the intersection; 2 scenes outside): **PASS —
  all 16 constraints**, evaluated on the 94 intersecting scenes.

## e. Binding constraints at 48 scenes (394 failing candidates)

Mean constraints dominate: Zip-NeRF LPIPS 281, Feature-Splatting LPIPS 269,
Feature-Splatting SSIM 252, Zip-NeRF SSIM 231, Zip-NeRF PSNR 197,
Instant-NGP SSIM 178, Feature-Splatting PSNR 164, Instant-NGP PSNR 152.
Every KS constraint binds less often (68–97). Full histogram in the JSON.

## Caveats

- Regime support on the intersection: {0: 724, 1: 417, 2: 457, 3: 715,
  4: 528, 5: 632} — minimum 417 ≥ 20 × b_max = 400, so balanced sampling is
  well-supported at every budget.
- This event uses the paper-table PSNR convention for Instant-NGP (`PNSR`,
  per-frame mean). The earlier exploratory `multi_method_frontier` key used
  the `psnr avgmse` column; joint counts differ slightly (e.g. 30 vs 32 at
  96 scenes). E1 supersedes it for rebuttal use.
- RASS-96 is evaluated on its 94 scenes inside the intersection; its 2
  scenes outside the intersection are excluded from all statistics.
- Seeds recorded: global 0; per-budget rng = default_rng(0 + b).
