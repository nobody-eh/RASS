## Why

The task requires recovering the exact 36-scene facility-location subset
certified in the baseline comparison. The repository contains the FL
*outcomes* but not the means to reconstruct the subset:

- `sweep_cluster_k/baseline_comparison_lpips_ks/baseline_min_size_summary.csv`
  — FL row: smallest size meeting the LCB target = 36, pass rate 0.1075
  (43/400), LCB 0.0808.
- `baseline_sweep_results.csv` — FL pass rate / LCB at sizes 12–96 (note:
  FL at 96 scenes drops to 0.0725 / LCB 0.051, below target).
- `baseline_eval_config.json` — M = 400, random_seed = 0, p_min = 0.08,
  paper tolerances, `facility_candidate_pool = 16`.

Missing (all searched exhaustively; also established in P0):

1. The facility-location selection **code** (objective, distance metric,
   greedy/perturbation algorithm) — not in the repo; audit scripts consume
   the summary CSV as "summary only".
2. The **per-run perturbation seeds** (or seed-derivation rule) for the 400
   FL runs.
3. Any exported FL **subset manifest** at any size.
4. A record of **which** of the 43 passing runs' subsets is "the" certified
   FL-36.

A from-scratch reimplementation cannot be validated: the only reference is
a stochastic pass rate (43/400) that depends on the unrecorded perturbation
protocol, so matching it would not certify the same subset, and per the
audit conventions we stop rather than improvise. Steps 2–8 (export table,
cross-method gaps, rank check, regime coverage, percentiles, 20-seed
stability, packaging) all depend on the recovered scene list and were not
executed.
